## The game
A pregnancy/delivery adventure in one long loop (all in `src/game.js`):
1. **Home** – phone-ringing cutscene in the bedroom, walk out to the ambulance (waypoint + star coins), ride inside it on the street "treadmill" (`Arriving in 2:00`, Speed Up).
2. **Arrival** – cutscene at the maternity hospital, walk to the entrance.
3. **Lobby check-in line** – queue, then the receptionist dialogue (baby name, twins offer).
4. **Waiting room line** + nurse heartbeat check, **breathing class line** + breathing minigame.
5. **Ultrasound wing line** + scan, **final line**, **delivery room** (doctor quiz, labor PUSH screen, baby reveal + rarity card, heading home).
- **Loop**: nap at home, the phone rings again, "SURPRISE BABY #N!", back to step 1.
Rooms are separate closed boxes placed far apart (`roomOrigin` in hospital.js) and swapped with a fade.

## Lines (the Don't Miss Your Flight line approach)
- **Real players only** (like the flight game's `PASSENGERS.bots: false`): `MOMS.bots` is false, so no bot moms stand in any line (main builds no NPC pool, `remote.botShare()` is 0, `LINES` is unused); the staff (receptionists, nurses, coach, sonographer, doctor, paramedic) are the only bots. Alone in a room the line is empty and you still walk the lanes to the head. The line HUD shows "N moms ahead of you" (an ETA only with bots). `MOMS.bots: true` brings back bot moms that fill in while real ones are few (`LINES` scaled by `botShare`, none from `MOMS.fullAt` real moms in the hospital rooms).
- `queue.js` `Queue`: the line is a distance `s` along the room's serpentine path (`linepath.js makePath`, 0 = the LINE STARTS HERE mat, `total` = the head). Entries, front first: `{ player: true, s }`, `{ remote, s }` (other real moms) and, with `MOMS.bots` only, `{ npc, s }` (this client's bot moms from `createNpcPool`, which expose x / y / z / active). Bots shuffle up (`QUEUE.lineSpeed`), never closer than `slotSpacing` to the one ahead, wait instead of walking into you (`lineStepClear`) and are solid (`lineBlockers.js`, `QUEUE.block`; real moms never are). Moms beyond 16 m skip the shadow pass.
- Joining (`QUEUE.joinRadius` of the mat) walks you along the lanes to your place (`player.autoTarget` carrot, `QUEUE.autoSpeed`, never past a real mom ahead); pushing the stick for `QUEUE.leaveHold` s steps out. The player is not frozen in line.
- The head goes to a free spot (`room.spots` in hospital.js: `lobby:0..2` = the three windows with `rec1..3`, `<room>:0` elsewhere) as soon as one is free (`QUEUE.realGap` apart; with bots one every `QUEUE.secondsPerPerson` s); everyone steps out ahead first (`leadOut`), bots wait `QUEUE.serve` s at the spot and leave via `room.exitRoute`. Offline you're sent when you reach the head; online the server calls you (`lineTurn` -> `callMe`; from 2nd / 3rd place too when enough spots are free, walking via the head of the line; with bots only from the head once the cooldown ran out) to the spot it hands you, and `syncRemoteLine` re-sorts the real moms (you + them) by server ticket. `game.playerTurn(id, spot, route)` walks the route, then that room's scene (`G.spotStaff` = the receptionist of your window).
- Cut Line: `QUEUE.cutLinePlaces` places (teleport up the lanes), online `cutLineOnServer(kind, past)` with the real moms passed. Kill (E): a bot spins off; a real mom gets `sendTroll('kill', her session)` (one relay per `NET.trollCooldown` s = the backend's `TROLL_COOLDOWN_MS`: `canTroll()` is checked before charging). Kill All: every bot ahead + `sendTroll('killAll', line)`. The receiver (`G.onTrolled`) checks it is aimed at her (Kill) or that she is ahead of the sender by server ticket (Kill All) and is knocked out: `ko` pose, Boxity death screen (`bloxity.deathScreen`, after `REMOTE.knockout` s), back at the room entrance to re-queue.

## Multiplayer (fellow moms)

## Rules
- Never sell anything for premium currency: every product costs in-game **coins** (`PRICES` in config.js), priced slightly high. Coins come from pickups, waiting, answers, breathing, phases and babies (`REWARDS`).
- Keep every original-looking asset procedural (canvas textures, code-built models); the reference screenshots are for layout and feel only.
- **No z-fighting**: never put two visible faces in the same plane. Ground layers have fixed heights (lawn -0.04 with depth bias, road 0.02, sidewalk 0.06, paths/mats 0.08, waypoint disc 0.12); decals go through `Builder.picture` (polygon offset); frames/trim must stand proud of the wall on every side. Camera near plane is 0.2 for depth precision.

## Detail vs. performance (keep both)
- **Characters** (`characters.js`): every NPC/staff is ONE `SkinnedMesh` (all parts merged, each vertex weighted to its bone) with ONE shared vertex-colour material whose map is a face atlas. Add detail with `kit.add(bone, geometry, colour, xf(...))`, never with extra meshes. Budget ~7k triangles per character; rounded boxes default to 1 segment. Moms beyond 16 m of the player skip the shadow pass (`queue.js`).
- **Static props**: build with `Builder`, then `bake()` the group (home, ambulance shell/interior/doors/wheels, street sections, hospital, rooms). `bake` keeps each part's own cast/receive flags (walls and ceilings must not cast, or rooms go dark). Anything that animates or swaps material (phone, crib mobile, light bars, monitors) stays out of the baked group.
- Measure with `__game.renderer.info` (autoReset off, one `renderer.render`): lobby ~200-250 draw calls incl. shadows.

- **Babydex** (`dex.js` logic, `hud.dex` window, `DEX` in config.js): 24 baby types in `characters.js` `BABY_TYPES` (keep the count = backend `DEX_TOTAL`; each new type's accessory goes in `BABY_EXTRAS`). `dex` { [type]: { n, s } } counts every baby ever kept (never cut like `babies`); rewards are claimed by key (`t:<type>:<tier>` stars at `DEX.tiers`, `r:<rarity>` rows, `all`) and pay coins only. The reveal (`reveal()` in game.js) rolls shiny (`DEX.shinyChance`, a `*` after the type in `carry`), applies the pity timer (`pity` deliveries without Epic+ -> `rollBabyType(rand, { minRarity })`) and the first-of-a-type bonus. Button under the babies counter (portrait: under Kill All), B key, Esc / backdrop closes. Babies animate (`buildBaby` -> `userData.animate(t)`: breathing, hands, shiny sparkles, per-type parts) - call it for carried, crib and remote babies.
- **Upgrades** (`upgrades.js` logic + effect numbers, `hud.upgrades` shop, `UPGRADES` in config.js; coins only, level 1 = `base`, each next x`grow`): Turbo Ambulance (`rideSeconds`, exhaust sparks + streaks), Comfy Shoes (`player.speedMult`, dust trail), Coin Magnet (`magnetReach`: coins fly in), Lullaby (`waitBonus` on ride / line ticks, notes + music box), Lucky Charm (`rollBabyType` `luck` -> `rarityWeights`, clover swirl), Proud Parent (`babyCoinMult`, coin fountain), Line Shield (`G.shields` per trip; `G.onTrolled` blocks a Kill with a fresnel bubble). Every effect has its own sound in audio.js; buying uses `spend(price, true)` + `sound.levelUp` so jingles never stack. Babydex + Upgrades share the `.rtools` column (a row on short landscape, under Kill All in portrait; on short touch screens Kill All sits 18 px lower to clear it), U key.
- `world.js` (lawn, street, houses, clouds, ride treadmill), `home.js` (bedroom + phone), `ambulance.js`, `hospital.js` (exterior + rooms), `props.js` (Builder with colliders, plants, stanchions, balloons), `paint.js` (canvas painters), `characters.js` (NPC/staff builder with knees/elbows, poses, NPC bellies, babies + rarities, pet Pip), `fx.js` (particles, confetti, rings), `audio.js` (all sound + music synthesized with WebAudio), `hud.js` / `hud.css` (all UI).
- Base kept from the template: `player.js` (now with `frozen` / `autoTarget`), `camera.js`, `input.js`, `touch.js`, `physics.js`, `lighting.js`, `postfx.js`, `bake.js`, `avatar/` (animator supports `pose` and `overlay`), `bloxity.js`.
- **Player belly** (`avatar/belly.js`, on you and every remote mom): built INTO the Bloxity torso, not a stuck-on ball. The torso is found by bone (skin weight on Spine1/Spine2; rigid meshes parented to them), its front is subdivided (edge splits by a position-only rule, no T-junctions) and pushed forward into a dome sized from that torso's own box (`BELLY` in config.js), so the player's shirt texture wraps it on the default body and any equipped torso part. Chest items (tagged `userData.avatarSlot` by loader.js) ride forward on the bump. Cached per source geometry; `breatheBelly` clones privately. No usable torso -> a sphere on Spine1 tinted from the torso texture. Apply it after loader.js swaps parts (`game.onAvatar` does).
- **Custom characters** (`avatar/customCharacter.js`): a whole-body .glb worn instead of the regular avatar. Local: `bloxity.getCharacter()`; other moms: `getCharacterFor(equipped)` (`characterId` rides in the look JSON's `equipped`, the backend passes it through). Same 22 bones / units as player.glb, so the animator, emotes and belly work unchanged; NO skin, body parts or proportions (`applyProportions` only sets the unit scale); items go on with `characterAccessoryMatrix(character, bone) * translate(slot.origin)` (`origin` in cdn.js), else `scale(rig.anchors[bone])` * the base-rig spot. Each glbUrl loads once and is cloned per wearer. Missing bones / a failed load -> the regular avatar (never hide the player). Dev: `__game.game.debug.wearCharacter({ id, glbUrl, rig, accessoryMatrix? })`, `null` to take it off.
